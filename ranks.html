HOW ADVANCEMENT WORKS

Promotion is earned through performance, discipline, and results. Your record follows you. Your reputation arrives first. The Empire loves a good success story—preferably one with fewer explosions than the last Death Star.

  • Training: Imperial Academy + specialization modules
  • Placement: Sector needs + your aptitude scores
  • Promotion: evaluations, commendations, and service time

IMPERIAL RANK LADDER

Ensign

Entry Officer

First leadership assignment. Learn to run a watch, follow doctrine, and avoid “creative interpretations” of orders.

  • Typical Role: Bridge Watch Officer
  • Focus: fundamentals, reliability

Lieutenant

Division Lead

Leads a shipboard division or ground unit. Responsible for readiness, reports, and keeping specialists aligned.

  • Typical Role: Flight Ops / Engineering Lead
  • Focus: execution, discipline

Commander

Operational Command

Owns outcomes. Runs multi-team operations and coordinates across systems, bases, and fleet assets.

  • Typical Role: Starship XO / Base Commander
  • Focus: outcomes, accountability

Captain

Ship or Installation CO

Commands a capital ship or critical installation. Your decisions have consequences…and paperwork.

  • Typical Role: Star Destroyer Captain
  • Focus: leadership, risk management

Admiral

Fleet Command

Directs campaigns, controls logistics, and decides where the Empire applies pressure (and where it simply arrives).

  • Typical Role: Sector Fleet Admiral
  • Focus: strategy, coordination

Moff

Sector Governor

Political authority with military teeth. Oversees systems, enforces compliance, and manages local governors.

  • Typical Role: Sector Governor
  • Focus: governance, stability

ROLE BRIEFS

Starship Systems Officer

Responsible for ship readiness: power, comms, shields, and damage response. You’ll lead specialists, run drills, and keep the Captain out of “surprise” scenarios.

Sector Compliance Coordinator

Works with local leadership to implement Imperial standards on taxation, security, and logistics. Heavy on policy, heavier on consequences.

Outer Rim Counter-Insurgency Analyst

Tracks rebel patterns, predicts supply movements, and coordinates interdiction operations. If you enjoy connecting dots, welcome home.

Rapid Response Trooper (RRT)

Deployed to stabilize incidents: riots, raids, facility breaches, and high-value recoveries. Expect fast movement and tighter rules.
